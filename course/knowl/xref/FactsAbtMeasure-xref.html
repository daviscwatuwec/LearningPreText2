Theorem 2. Facts about outer measure.

  1. (points have no measure) For any \(x_0\in \R\text{,}\) \(m^*(\{x_0\})=0\)
    🔗
    🔗
  2. (monotonicity) If \(U\subseteq V\) then \(m^*(U)\le m^*(V)\)
    🔗
    🔗
  3. (countable subadditivity) For any countable collection of sets \(X_1,X_2,\dots\text{,}\) \(m^*\left(\cup_k X_k\right)\le \Sum_k m^*(X_k)\)
