Lemma 13.

Let \(\alpha\gt0\text{,}\) \(f:[a,b]\to \R\) be bounded and satisfy that \(\osc(f,x)\lt \alpha\text{.}\) For any \(\epsilon\gt0\text{,}\) there is a single \(\delta\gt0\) so that for every intervals \(I\subseteq [a,b]\) with length \(\ell(I)\lt \delta\text{,}\) \(\osc(f,I)\lt\alpha+\epsilon\)
